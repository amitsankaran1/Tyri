2.4 (7) — Actionable blocking warnings

Blocks that need attention now appear in a dedicated section near the top of Focus. Open the named block for a What to do section with cause-specific instructions, direct links to its app lists, Retry blocking, Screen Time settings and diagnostics. Schedule, automatic-end and usage-tracking errors are included. Retrying preserves healthy usage allowances.

Please check the block that previously showed Needs attention without instructions. Follow its recovery steps and confirm whether blocking or tracking resumes. If the warning persists, open Blocking diagnostics from the same screen and share the report with the iOS version and affected block/app names.

This build retains the state-machine reliability fixes from 2.4 (6). Real-device Screen Time enforcement still requires acceptance testing. AI/NL features remain deferred.
