Tyri 2.3 (7): Clearer blocking status and usage-limit setup.

- Focus now distinguishes Tracking usage (blue), Blocking on (red), On a break (amber), and Blocking off.
- Verify a usage limit shows Tracking usage before the limit is reached. At the limit, it should switch to Blocking on with an end time.
- Pause one block while another is blocking: the overall status must remain Blocking on.
- Usage-limit setup now explains: use apps for the allowance, block them for the configured duration, then repeat. Unused usage resets at midnight.
- Live minutes-used totals are not included in this build. The app displays “Usage so far unavailable”; the numbers shown in the design review were sample data only.
- Confirm active blocks still prevent weakening settings, unchanged Save preserves the usage budget, and existing lists/schedules/NFC settings survive the upgrade.
- Confirm usage shields remain through app relaunch and lock/unlock, and breaks resume at the requested time.

Please report the block type, its settings, and whether Tyri was open when an issue occurred.
