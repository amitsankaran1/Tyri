Tyri 2.3 (9): Repeat usage lockouts and quick temporary blocks.

- Complete two usage cycles: reach your allowance, stay blocked for the full lockout, then reach the allowance again. Repeat with Tyri closed and after reopening it.
- Focus shows registration failures with Retry block. Reopening retries failed usage registrations without replacing a healthy running allowance.
- In Focus, tap Block for a while, choose a list and duration, then start. The session ends automatically and creates no saved rule. Custom times run from 15 minutes to 24 hours in 15-minute steps.
- Close Tyri during a temporary session and confirm expiry still works. Changing the source list does not change the session already running.
- Stop early starts a five-minute wait. Reopening or finishing the wait does not release automatically; tap Release block when the wait completes.
- Other active blocks still apply after a temporary session ends. Emergency Access clears temporary sessions too.
- Existing lists, schedules, NFC settings and blocking restrictions should survive the upgrade.
- Live numeric usage totals remain unavailable. Please report block settings, exact times, build number and whether Tyri was open if blocking ends early or fails to repeat.
