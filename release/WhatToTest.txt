Tyri 2.3 (5): easier scheduling and clearer access settings, plus recovery of overnight scheduled blocking.

New interface:
- Create block lists Schedule first, Usage limit second, and NFC tag third.
- Schedule: choose start/end times, use Every day/Weekdays/Weekends shortcuts, or tap individual days. Overnight schedules explain that they end the next day.
- Stop early: choose whether to allow it and how long to wait before stopping.
- Take a break: choose whether to allow a temporary pause and its length. Blocking resumes automatically; other active blocks still apply.
- Check custom days and saved settings after reopening the app.

Please check on a physical iPhone:
- Update without deleting the app; saved lists and rules should remain.
- During a daily 20:00–10:00 schedule, open Tyri and confirm the rule is active and selected apps (including Instagram) show a shield.
- Force-quit and reopen Tyri during that window; blocking should remain active.
- A Friday overnight schedule should continue into Saturday morning, ending at its configured time.
- Reopening during a break must not shorten the break or grant another one.
- After an early-disable cooldown or emergency release, reopening must preserve release until the next schedule window.
- With Tyri closed, check automatic start/end on device.
